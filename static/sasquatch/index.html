An honest, capable trailer

The Smuggler is built by Sasquatch Campers in Silverton, Colorado — a small shop known for lightweight, all-aluminum construction and serious off-road capability. This is the top-tier PRO-X build with the full electrical, water, kitchen, and storage upgrades, plus a 23Zero rooftop tent, 270° awning, and shower system. We simply don't have time to use it, and with our other overlanding vehicles it deserves an owner who will put it to work.

Why the price makes sense

Sasquatch trailers are hand-built to order with a typical 6–12 month wait. A comparable PRO-X build with these same accessories prices out around $56,000+ new today — and you'd wait the better part of a year for it. This one is ready now, in excellent condition, at a fair discount off new.
